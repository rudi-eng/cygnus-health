# Cygnus brand direction

The owner’s priority is a **beauty concept for hair and skin**, built around a living aloe plant. Hair and skin-care ideas should be prominent in the homepage, shop story and studio. Growing, plant care and the passport support that beauty journey.

Preserve the warm botanical visual identity, bilingual Turkish/English experience, 7 cm plastic nursery pot, and small linked Aslana Intelligence footer credit.

Beauty ideas can be presented as clearly labelled development concepts. The actual plant’s species and cosmetic suitability are not confirmed. Do not turn concept titles into DIY application instructions, efficacy claims or included-cosmetic promises without the appropriate identification, formulation and safety review. The current kit contains a young living plant and educational materials.
