## Book and recipe-section scope

Working title: **The Aloe Recipe Book / Aloe Tarif Kitabı**.

1. **Meet the ingredient:** correct plant identification, clear inner-leaf gel versus yellow latex and rind, and why a young kit plant is not ready for harvesting.
2. **Preparation:** a practical gel-preparation method for an appropriately identified, suitable mature plant; clean utensils, handling, and the limits of home preparation.
3. **Face and beauty:** simple external cosmetic applications, with ingredient suitability and practical directions assessed before publication.
4. **Hair:** actual rinse-out or conditioning routines, with realistic cosmetic descriptions rather than hair-growth or scalp-treatment promises.
5. **Hands and body:** straightforward external care routines using reviewed ingredients and directions.
6. **Freshness and hygiene:** small quantities, contamination prevention, and no unvalidated refrigerator, freezer or long-storage shelf-life claims.
7. **Medical evidence:** a separate, referenced explanation of what has been studied, the limitations of those studies, and when professional care is needed. Research involving prepared aloe products does not establish that raw gel from the kit treats a medical condition. This chapter must not become home-treatment instructions for burns, wounds, infections or other diseases.
8. **Grow your future ingredient:** plant care, maturity and appropriate harvesting guidance, supporting rather than replacing the recipe book.

Every published recipe should contain a clear purpose, a realistic description of its expected cosmetic benefit, the suitable ingredient/source, amounts, equipment, preparation, directions for use, relevant precautions, freshness instructions, and evidence/review notes. Avoid replacing useful recipes with vague inspiration titles.

## Ingredient clarification

On 22 October 2026, the owner answered **“Confirmed as Aloe vera.”** Record this as grower identification, not independent laboratory certification or confirmation that any individual batch is sterile or suitable for a particular customer. The original photographs are not in this workspace.

The young 7 cm kit plant is not a ready-to-harvest ingredient. Fresh-leaf directions are for an appropriately identified, suitable mature Aloe vera. Home cosmetic recipes are educational and unvalidated formulations; do not present them as medical treatments. A finished cosmetic aloe product has its own label directions and must not silently be treated as interchangeable with fresh household gel.
